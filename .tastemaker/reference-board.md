## Quality bar
- Ciao Energy: persistent product and changing evidence in one coordinated story.
- SONIQ: authored product image becomes the next visual world.

## Borrow
- Material: generated ignition/reveal pair -> dark red metallic product cinema.
- Layout: automotive editorial -> dramatic changes between close, wide and detail framing.
- Motion: Ciao/SONIQ -> transitions tied to what the visitor learns.
- Asset language: Honda official imagery -> factual feature and color proof.

## Avoid
- V1 Honda/SHIFT global fixed stage with chapter panels.
- Generic vehicle configurator floating in an empty dark void for the entire page.
- Fake repayments, stock, ratings, testimonials or showroom claims.
- Moving a single transparent scooter cutout through every section.

## Direction contract
- Thesis: Make an ordinary day feel chosen; make the next ownership step simple.
- First viewport: ignition encounter with immediate dealer identity and call action.
- System: cream/red/cobalt campaign sequence, hard editorial cuts, one bounded hero scrub.
- Risk: generated imagery can drift from the exact Dio or read as the real branch.
