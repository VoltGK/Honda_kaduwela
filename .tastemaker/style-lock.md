# Honda Kaduwela V2 style lock

## Direction
- Thesis: The Dio makes an ordinary day feel personally chosen; Macro makes ownership approachable.
- Mode: Persuade + Experience.
- Dials: variance 9, motion 8, density 4, art direction 9.
- First viewport: dark ignition portrait, oversized campaign line in the image's negative space, immediate call action.
- Risk: generated campaign imagery must never replace official product proof or imply the fictional showroom is real.

## Color contract
- Text `#18110e`; background `#f5eee4`; surface `#fffaf4`.
- Primary red `#c91424`; cobalt `#06437f`; border `#8d8177`; on-primary `#ffffff`.
- Text-safe: text/on-primary, text/surface, text/background, cobalt/white, surface/cobalt,
  background/cobalt, red/white, surface/red, background/red, text/border.
- Measured with tastemaker check_contrast.py; all named text pairings >= 4.5:1.

## Type and shape
- Display: Archivo Black, uppercase, tight and slightly italic only at campaign peaks.
- Body/interface: Archivo Variable; 18px body and 16px information floors.
- Square editorial controls, hairline rules, clipped image apertures. No pills or nested cards.

## Structure
- Macrostructure: Long-Scroll Narrative.
- Nav N5 side rail; hero H3 photographic fold; features F1 alternating bands;
  CTA C2 statement/action; footer Ft2 inline.
- Arc: encounter -> ignition/reveal -> ride -> physical feature proof -> choose color ->
  ownership paths -> handover/trust -> care -> local action.

## Motion
- One bounded sticky hero; scroll expands the lit reveal from the headlamp region.
- Later scenes remain in normal document flow with image parallax and editorial reveals.
- Choice controls respond immediately; no global fixed stage, scroll hijacking, or continuous idle animation.
- Reduced motion resolves to the final hero portrait and ordinary document flow.

## Asset cast
- Hero anchor: Gemini2 ignition/reveal pair.
- Mode range: city ride, cobalt everyday stop, bright handover, clinical service.
- Product proof: official Honda feature photographs and transparent color assets.
- Human proof: none claimed; generated people are concept imagery.
- Texture: metallic paint, road blur, seat/key, tiled showroom and service tools.
- Micro assets: color swatches, route numerals, typographic arrows.
- Rejected: V1 generated road/showroom world and global fixed-canvas mechanism.
