# Honda Kaduwela asset intake

Put untouched source files in this directory. Do not resize, remove backgrounds, rename
models, or compress them before handoff. A phone photo at full resolution is more useful
than a pre-edited low-resolution social export.

## Minimum pilot pack

### 1. Macro identity

- `macro-logo.svg` preferred, or `macro-logo.png` with transparency and at least 1600px
  on its longest edge.
- Any dealer-brand rules or a clear confirmation that the logo may be used in the private
  customer pitch.

### 2. Hero scooter

Confirm the exact model and variant currently sold by this branch. Dio 125 H-Smart is the
working recommendation because it appears in the current public feed, but it must be
confirmed before the site states availability or price.

Best handoff, in order:

1. a licensed accurate `.glb` plus textures;
2. 24–48 consistent turntable frames on one background;
3. a short high-resolution turntable video with a steady camera;
4. at least three full-resolution photographs: front three-quarter, side and rear
   three-quarter, shot at the same height/light;
5. one clean high-resolution front-three-quarter product image for the still pilot.

Suggested names:

```text
hero-dio125-front-3q.jpg
hero-dio125-side.jpg
hero-dio125-rear-3q.jpg
hero-dio125-turntable.mp4
hero-dio125.glb
```

Do not send screenshots containing browser chrome, price labels laid over the bike, or
images where wheels/bodywork are cropped.

### 3. The real branch

- `macro-storefront-wide.jpg` — straight or slightly angled exterior, full storefront.
- `macro-showroom-wide.jpg` — broad showroom view with clean floor and useful depth.
- `macro-service-bay-wide.jpg` — a real service area in active but tidy condition.
- `macro-team.jpg` — optional staff/owner image with permission from people shown.
- `trade-old-bike.jpg` — optional old motorcycle, isolated against a simple background.

Use originals at 2400px or wider where available. Shoot horizontal and vertical versions
if the phone/camera allows; avoid portrait mode blur around wheels and mirrors.

## Facts to confirm in one message

- primary WhatsApp number;
- primary call number;
- full weekly opening hours and Sunday status;
- currently stocked models and colors;
- whether trade-in is currently available and how its first assessment works;
- what financing/leasing wording may be used without implying approval;
- service, genuine-parts and after-sales claims the branch approves;
- whether published model prices may appear in the pitch;
- whether English only is acceptable for the pilot, or who approves Sinhala copy;
- permission status for the Macro/Honda/product/branch assets supplied.

Public Facebook and Google Maps images remain research evidence until their use is
confirmed. If customer assets cannot arrive before the pitch, the alternative is an
explicitly private concept-art pilot whose imagery is labelled as visualization and whose
inventory/pricing claims remain absent.
