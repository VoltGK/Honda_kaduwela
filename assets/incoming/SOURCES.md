# Pilot asset sources and use boundary

All files in this intake area are source material for a **private, non-production customer
pitch prototype**. They are not evidence of current branch inventory, customer approval,
or permission for public deployment. Replace or explicitly clear them before any public
launch.

```text
Use case: stylized-concept

Scene/backdrop: tropical Sri Lankan urban roadside in bright late-morning light; a clean asphalt road begins close to camera, its pale center route line curves through the frame and resolves into a bold red-and-white architectural showroom portal in the middle distance; subtle local concrete, roadside greenery, utility geometry and humid atmospheric depth; showroom interior only suggested beyond the threshold.
Subject: environment only. Leave a clean grounded area in the center-right foreground where an authentic scooter cutout will later be composited.
Style/medium: high-end photoreal automotive campaign photography with restrained cinematic art direction, believable lens and materials, crisp but natural.
Composition/framing: 16:9 landscape, low knee-height camera, deep perspective; left third quiet and low-detail for large headline copy; center-right foreground open for the scooter; road line must remain legible behind that product area and guide the eye toward the portal; useful edge bleed for ultrawide cropping.
Lighting/mood: energetic tropical daylight, cobalt-blue sky, warm sun, controlled red reflections near the portal, confident and welcoming rather than dark luxury.
Color palette: asphalt charcoal, tropical green, cobalt sky, sharp Honda-like red and clean white architecture, small warm-yellow road accents.
Constraints: no vehicles of any kind, no motorcycles, no people, no logos, no brand marks, no signs, no readable text, no flags, no pricing, no watermark; no fake business identity; retain generous copy-safe negative space on the left and a clean product footprint on the right.
```
